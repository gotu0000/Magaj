**Gist:** Plot $x$ against $t$ and the whole motion becomes one curve. Height is where the object is; steepness is how fast it is going.

**Why it matters:** It turns [[Instantaneous velocity|velocity]] into something you can see — the slope of the tangent at time $t$. Rising curve, positive velocity; flat, zero; falling, negative. The five sampled times are the ones [[Average velocity|average velocity]] is worked through on p.1, and the ordering is what makes those cases come out: $x_{t_3} > x_{t_4} > x_{t_2} > x_{t_1} = x_{t_5}$.
