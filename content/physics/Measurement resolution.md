**Gist:** The quantity you want to measure dictates the instrument you need. Fix the geometry and the required precision follows from it.

**Why it matters:** To clock a bullet slower than sound ($< 300$ m/s) across a gate of $148.5 \pm 0.5$ mm, the crossing lasts roughly half a millisecond — so the timer has to resolve at least $1/10$ th of a millisecond. The apparatus is not a free choice; the physics sets its specification.
