**Gist:** The quantity you want to measure dictates the instrument you need. Fix the geometry and the required precision follows from it.

**Why it matters:** To clock a bullet slower than sound ($< 300$ m/s) across a gate of $148.5 \pm 0.5$ cm, even the fastest crossing lasts about $5$ ms — so a timer resolving $1/10$ th of a millisecond pins it down to roughly $2\%$. The apparatus is not a free choice; the physics sets its specification.

> [!warning] Source unit
> p.3 writes the gate distance in **mm**. The intended unit is **cm** — an error in the handwritten note, not a transcription slip. At $148.5$ mm the fastest crossing would last only ${\sim}0.5$ ms, which $1/10$ ms timing could not resolve usefully.
