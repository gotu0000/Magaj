**Gist:** Light a falling object with a strobe at a known rate, and every flash marks a known instant. The spacing between flashes is then a distance you can measure.

**Why it matters:** A $2$ Hz strobe gives two ticks per second, so the time between images is known exactly. Distance between flashes plus that known time gives speed; with $g$ known it also pins down where in the fall the object was, and so the depth. The lecture calls this "like a vision problem".
