**Gist:** The unsigned value of [[Instantaneous velocity|instantaneous velocity]] — magnitude kept, direction discarded.

**Why it matters:** It is what a speedometer reads. Unlike [[Average speed|average speed]], which needs an interval, it is defined at a single instant.

> [!warning] Source wording
> p.3 reads "instantaneous speed is non signed value of **average** velocity". It should be *instantaneous* velocity. Confirmed as a slip in the source, not a transcription error.
