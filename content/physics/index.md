## Concepts

%% Regenerate after each lecture: one line per concept note at the physics/ root, alphabetical. Split into sub-headings only when the flat list stops being scannable — ask first. %%

- [[Average acceleration]] — change in velocity over the time it took.
- [[Average speed]] — distance over time, unsigned.
- [[Average velocity]] — displacement over time, signed.
- [[Free fall]] — everything falls at $g = 9.8$ m/s², whatever its mass or shape.
- [[Instantaneous acceleration]] — $dv/dt$, the second derivative of position.
- [[Instantaneous speed]] — the unsigned value of instantaneous velocity.
- [[Instantaneous velocity]] — $dx/dt$, the tangent slope on a position-time graph.
- [[Kinematic equations]] — constant acceleration makes position a quadratic in $t$.
- [[Measurement resolution]] — what you want to measure sets the instrument you need.
- [[Measuring g by free fall]] — drop from a known height, time it, solve for $g$.
- [[Position-time graph]] — the whole motion as a single curve.
- [[Sign convention]] — which way counts as $+x$ is a choice, not a fact.
- [[Stroboscopic measurement]] — a known flash rate turns spacing into speed.
