**Gist:** Near the surface everything falls with the same acceleration, $a = g = 9.8$ m/s², independent of mass and shape.

$$x = x_0 + v_0 t + \tfrac{1}{2} g t^2 \qquad v = v_0 + g t \qquad a = g$$

**Why it matters:** A special case of [[Kinematic equations|constant acceleration]] where the constant is handed to you by the planet. The independence from mass and shape is an experimental fact, not something derived.

> [!question] Can it be proved?
> The lecture's answer: "Not through first principle."
