**Gist:** [[Average velocity]] taken over a shrinking interval. In the limit it becomes the derivative of position with respect to time.

$$V_t = \lim_{\Delta t \to 0} \frac{x_{t + \Delta t} - x_t}{\Delta t} = \frac{dx}{dt}$$

**Why it matters:** This is where calculus enters kinematics. On a [[Position-time graph|position-time graph]] it is the tangent slope at time $t$, so its sign — $> 0$, $= 0$, $< 0$ — reads straight off the curve.
