**Gist:** Change in velocity divided by the time it took.

$$\bar a = \frac{\Delta v}{\Delta t}$$

**Why it matters:** A small $\Delta t$ makes $\bar a$ enormous, which is why impacts break things. A tennis ball reversing from $-5$ to $+5$ m/s in about $10^{-2}$ s averages

$$\bar a = \frac{5 - (-5)}{10^{-2}} = 10^3\ \text{m/s}^2$$
