**Gist:** Total distance covered divided by the time taken. Always positive — it carries no direction.

$$\text{average speed} = \frac{\text{distance}}{\text{time}}$$

**Why it matters:** This is what everyday language calls "speed", and it is *not* the magnitude of [[Average velocity|average velocity]]. An object that returns to where it started has zero average velocity but non-zero average speed.
