---
title: Measuring g by free fall
tags: [physics, measurement]
status: seed
---

**Gist:** Drop something from a known height $h$, time the fall, and $g$ falls out of the arithmetic.

Released from rest ($x_0 = 0$, $v_0 = 0$) the drop gives $h = \tfrac{1}{2} g t^2$, so

$$t = c \sqrt{\frac{h}{g}} \qquad c = \sqrt{2}$$

**Why it matters:** Two measurable quantities — one length and one time — yield a constant that cannot be derived from first principles. From $h = 3$ m the fall takes about $0.8$ s, and the uncertainty in $g$ follows from the uncertainties in $h$ and $t$.
