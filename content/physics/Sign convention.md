**Gist:** Which direction counts as positive $x$ is chosen, not discovered. Fix it once at the start, and every sign that follows is relative to that choice — in the lecture's words, "just the convention, my free choice".

**Why it matters:** [[Average velocity|Velocity]] and [[Instantaneous acceleration|acceleration]] only have signs *because* an axis was picked. Flip the axis and every sign flips with it, while the physics stays the same.
